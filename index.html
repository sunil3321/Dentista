What are the working hours of Dentista Clinic in Urapakkam?
Dentista is open Monday to Saturday from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM in the morning, and 5:00 PM to 8:00 PM in the evening. Sunday is closed.
Are dental crown and veneer procedures painful?
Not at all! Dr. D Deepa uses localized painless anesthesia techniques to ensure you feel zero discomfort during crown preparation or veneer placement.
